Option A

Dedicated section — "Who's behind this"

New numbered block (05, after Record) built from the same pillar-card component already used in Vision. Reads as a discrete, skimmable credibility stop — visitors who want the social proof go looking for it; visitors who don't, aren't interrupted.

Best if: endorsements are few but weighty (named colleagues, past CS Presidents) and deserve their own moment.

Option B

Sprinkled — one quote per relevant section, plus a name-chip strip in the hero

No new section. Each existing block (Who / Vision / Record) gets a single pull-quote from someone who can speak directly to that specific claim, styled with the page's existing .pull blockquote treatment. A compact strip of endorser chips sits in the hero, under the credentials chips.

Best if: you want endorsements to reinforce specific claims ("here's proof for what I just said") rather than read as a separate testimonials wall — but it needs one good quote per section, or the gaps show.

Option C

Hybrid — hero credibility strip + full endorsements section

A thin, low-key strip of names sits right under the hero actions (skimmed by everyone, costs one line), and it deep-links to a full section further down for anyone who wants the actual quotes. Gets both jobs done: instant credibility signal at the top of the page, full-length testimony available without cluttering the fold.

Best if: you're not sure yet whether endorsements are a headline feature or a supporting detail — this hedges, and it's the easiest of the three to trim back later.
